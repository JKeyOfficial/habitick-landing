Hey, I'm Jacob

I'm an apprentice IT Technician, amateur software developer, and the creator of HabiTick. I find keeping routines difficult; I always start something with the intention of doing it regularly, but life always seems to get in the way, and within a week I've lost the habit. I built HabiTick to help me avoid this cycle, and create a clean, guilt-free habit tracker and daily routine planner to help me build sustainable habits without the anxiety of broken streaks.

Outside of work, I enjoy running, football (I'm an Arsenal fan), gaming, and all things tech. I play the guitar, and have been learning Spanish too. These are all things I'm trying to keep consistent with using HabiTick.

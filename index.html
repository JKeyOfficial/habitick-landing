FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about HabiTick, guilt-free routines, pricing, and how it compares to apps like TickTick.

What makes HabiTick the best minimalist habit tracker app? ▼
HabiTick combines habit tracking and daily task management in one minimalist, guilt-free interface. Unlike traditional habit apps that penalise you for missed days, HabiTick includes Smart Streaks, Streak Shields, and 48-hour backdating so your real progress is always protected.
Why is HabiTick a top TickTick alternative? ▼
TickTick and other productivity tools require expensive recurring monthly or yearly subscriptions. HabiTick offers a clean, focused habit tracker and task app starting from 99p/mo or a single lifetime payment of £12.99 — pay monthly or own it forever. Read our full HabiTick vs TickTick comparison.
How do Streak Shields work? ▼
Streak Shields are earned automatically by building consistent habits and completing tasks. If life gets busy or you take an unexpected rest day, a Streak Shield protects your hard-earned streak from resetting to zero.
Can I use HabiTick on Web, Android, and iOS? ▼
Yes! HabiTick works in any web browser at app.habitick.app, is available as a native app on the Google Play Store for Android, and installs instantly on iOS (iPhone & iPad) via Safari 'Add to Home Screen'.
Can I track flexible or weekly habits without breaking my streak? ▼
Yes. HabiTick supports flexible scheduling and rest days. If a habit is scheduled 3 days a week, your off-days will never break your streak or trigger guilt.
Is HabiTick ADHD-friendly? ▼
Yes! HabiTick was specifically designed with neurodivergent and ADHD workflows in mind: zero clutter, guilt-free streak recovery with Streak Shields, 48-hour backdating, and a combined daily view for both tasks and habits.
Is HabiTick free to use? ▼
Yes! The Free plan gives you unlimited habit tracking, to-do task management, smart streak tracking, completion analytics, holiday mode, and daily mood journaling. Upgrading to Pro (99p/mo or £12.99 lifetime) unlocks 5 Streak Shields and personalized AI insights powered by Google Gemini.
